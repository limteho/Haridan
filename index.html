    Corporate Governance Framework

    About Integrity & Whistleblowing Policy

    Haridan Corporate Solutions Sdn. Bhd. maintains a zero-tolerance stance against bribery, corruption, fraud, and unethical conduct in line with Malaysian corporate governance standards and internal compliance procedures.

    Scope of Reportable Conduct

    • Offering, giving, receiving, or soliciting any gratification or bribe
    • Undisclosed conflicts of interest in vendor or supplier selection
    • Misappropriation of company funds, assets, or procurement records
    • Abuse of authority, intimidation, or workplace misconduct

    Whistleblower Safeguards

    • Optional anonymous submission without mandatory identity fields
    • Restricted access limited to authorised Integrity & Compliance officers
    • Strict prohibition against retaliation, demotion, or harassment
    • Objective fact-finding and documented review for every submission
